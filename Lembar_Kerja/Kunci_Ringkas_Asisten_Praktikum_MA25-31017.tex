\begin{chapterbox}{\color{white}\bfseries Bab 5}\end{chapterbox}
\textbf{B5.1.} \(f'(x)=\frac{3}{2}\sqrt{x}-7\).\\
\textbf{B5.2.} Di \(x=1.5\): maju \(\approx-5.017381\), mundur \(\approx-5.325765\), pusat \(\approx-5.171573\).\\
\textbf{B5.3.} Nilai eksak \(f'(1.5)\approx-5.162883\). Galat absolut: maju \(\approx0.14550\), mundur \(\approx0.16288\), pusat \(\approx0.00869\).\\
\textbf{B5.4.} Untuk \(f(x)=\ln x\), \(f'(x)=1/x\), jadi \(f'(2)=0.5\). Beda pusat \(h=0.25\): \(\approx0.502628\); galat relatif \(\approx0.5256\%\).\\
\textbf{B5.5.} Inti jawaban: trade-off \(h\) antara truncation dan round-off.

\textbf{B6.5.} Jika antiderivatif diketahui, galat sebenarnya bisa dihitung; jika tidak, pakai pembandingan antarmetode atau refinement.
